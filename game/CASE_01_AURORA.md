# Case 01 — The Aurora Theatre Fire

Short design reference for the previously drafted test story; the origin of the
anomaly and the final continuity are not established canon.

In 2026, Marina Azevedo (54), manager of Teatro Aurora, complains that the
municipal registry shows a second emergency exit that does not exist. Old
photographs show a door, yet no masonry work explains its disappearance. An
inspection threatens to close the theatre. A fire occurred there on 17 July 2021.

Paula (28) survived with a scar. In the currently recognized version, Jonas
(30) rescued her and they became partners. Augusto (67) remembers the emergency
door and mentions Eva, but cannot remember who she is. Marina denies knowing
Eva; records suggest Eva Azevedo was Marina's erased sister. A 19:42 phone call
from the theatre to Jonas carries Eva's voice: “A Paula tá lá dentro”.

Evidence to encounter later includes old door photographs, building and masonry
records, the call recording, a cup, a musical instrument, a blurred photograph,
Marina's letter, a rescue photograph, and Jonas's bus records and contradictory
accident records involving Ricardo Almeida.

Two incompatible causal continuities:

- **Recognized:** Eva is absent; Jonas saves Paula through the main entrance
  and remains in her life.
- **Alternative:** Eva exists, opens the emergency door and saves Paula; Jonas
  does not enter and continues his bus journey involving an accident with
  Ricardo.

Rectification must reconstruct visual causal sequences and force a meaningful
continuity decision, without canonizing the anomaly's origin or prematurely
choosing one definitive ending. In the test-story epilogue, the following
Sunday Marina, Paula and Jonas seem normal; a door that should not exist closes
in the dressing-room corridor.

Tone: 90% administrative normality, 10% unsettling strangeness. Institutional
mystery, not explicit cosmic exposition.
